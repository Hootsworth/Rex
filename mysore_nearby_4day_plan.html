4-Day Itinerary: Discover Spots Near Mysore

Embark on an exciting 4-day journey to explore the captivating tourist spots surrounding Mysore, featuring historical towns, stunning waterfalls, and lush hill stations.

Day 1

  • Srirangapatna (18 km from Mysore)
  • Brindavan Gardens (20 km from Mysore)
  • Somnathpur Temple (35 km from Mysore)

Day 2

  • Shivanasamudra Falls (70 km from Mysore)
  • Talakadu (50 km from Mysore)

Day 3

  • Coorg (Madikeri) (120 km from Mysore)

Day 4

  • Shravanabelagola (80 km from Mysore)
  • Nagarhole National Park (90 km from Mysore)
