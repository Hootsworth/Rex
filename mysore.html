4-Day Itinerary for Tourist Spots Near Mysore

Day 1: Historical & Gardens (Close to Mysore)

Morning (Srirangapatna - 18 km from Mysore):

  • Visit Tipu Sultan's Summer Palace.
  • Explore Srirangapatna Fort.
  • Visit Ranganathaswamy Temple.
  • This island town offers rich religious, cultural, and historical significance.

Afternoon (Brindavan Gardens - 20 km from Mysore):

  • Enjoy the symmetrical gardens and meticulously manicured lawns.
  • Experience the musical fountain show in the evening.
  • Consider boating on River Cauvery.

Day 2: Waterfalls & Wildlife (Moderately Further)

Morning (Shivanasamudra Falls - Approximately 75 km from Mysore):

  • Witness the stunning Gaganachukki and Barachukki waterfalls formed by the River Cauvery.
  • Enjoy the scenic views and auditory experience.

Afternoon (Bandipur National Park - Approximately 80 km from Mysore):

  • Go for a safari to spot wildlife (tigers, elephants, deer, etc.).
  • Part of the Nilgiri Biosphere Reserve, offering a rich wildlife experience.

Day 3: Hill Station & Nature (A Bit Further)

Full Day (Coorg / Madikeri - Approximately 120 km from Mysore):

  • Explore the "Scotland of India" with its lush coffee plantations, misty hills, and waterfalls.
  • Visit Abbey Falls and Raja's Seat for panoramic views.
  • Explore local spice plantations.
  • Consider a visit to Namdroling Monastery (Golden Temple) in Bylakuppe.

Day 4: Heritage & Serenity (Cultural Exploration)

Morning (Shravanabelagola - Approximately 80 km from Mysore):

  • Visit the colossal statue of Gomateshwara (Bahubali).
  • A significant Jain pilgrimage site with historical importance and breathtaking views from the hilltop.

Afternoon (Talakad - Approximately 45 km from Mysore):

  • Explore the ancient temples buried in sand.
  • Visit the Vaidyanatheshwara Temple and other temples dedicated to Lord Shiva.
  • Enjoy the serene riverside environment of River Cauvery.
