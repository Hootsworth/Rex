# Tourist Spots Near Shringeri & 4-Day Plan

Here are some good tourist spots near Shringeri, categorized by distance:

## One-Day Trip Spots (Within 40 km):
*   **Kigga Temple** – 10 km
*   **Sirimane Falls** – 15 km – Beautiful waterfall ideal for quick nature visits
*   **Agumbe** – 24 km – Rainforest, sunset view, and nature trails
*   **Kundadri Hills** – 32 km – Hilltop Jain temple with panoramic views
*   **Hariharapura Mutt** – 25 km
*   **Kavishaila** – 38 km – Hilltop dedicated to poet Kuvempu

## Longer Trip Options (Over 40 km):
*   **Kavaledurga Fort** – 42 km
*   **Kollur Mookambika Temple** – 75 km
*   **Udupi Sri Krishna Temple** – 81 km
*   **Malpe Beach** – 103 km
*   **Siganduru Chowdeshwari Temple** – 85 km

## 4-Day Plan for Shringeri & Nearby Tourist Spots

This plan is based on the above spots, focusing on a mix of spiritual, nature, and cultural experiences, with Shringeri as your base:

### Day 1: Shringeri & Local Temples
*   **Morning:** Arrive in Shringeri, check into your accommodation.
*   **Afternoon:** Visit the famous **Sringeri Sharadamba Temple** and the Vidyashankara Temple within the complex. Spend time absorbing the spiritual ambiance.
*   **Evening:** Explore the local market in Shringeri.
*   **Optional:** Visit **Kigga Temple** (10 km) if you have extra time and interest in more local temples.

### Day 2: Nature & Views
*   **Morning:** Head to **Sirimane Falls** (15 km). Enjoy the beautiful waterfall and the surrounding nature. You can spend a couple of hours here.
*   **Afternoon:** Drive to **Agumbe** (24 km from Shringeri). Explore the rainforest area and enjoy the panoramic sunset view from the Agumbe Sunset Point.
*   **Evening:** Return to Shringeri.

### Day 3: Hilltop & History
*   **Morning:** Visit **Kundadri Hills** (32 km from Shringeri). Explore the hilltop Jain temple and enjoy the scenic panoramic views.
*   **Afternoon:** Head towards **Kavishaila** (38 km from Shringeri), a serene hilltop dedicated to the renowned Kannada poet Kuvempu. Alternatively, if interested in history and a moderate trek, visit **Kavaledurga Fort** (42 km from Shringeri). This will be a longer excursion.
*   **Evening:** Return to Shringeri.

### Day 4: Coastal Excursion (Longer Trip)
*   **Full Day Trip:** Embark on a longer day trip towards the coast.
    *   **Option A (Spiritual & Coastal):** Visit **Kollur Mookambika Temple** (75 km) in the morning, a highly revered pilgrimage site. Then proceed to **Udupi Sri Krishna Temple** (81 km) in the afternoon.
    *   **Option B (Beach & Culture):** Visit **Udupi Sri Krishna Temple** (81 km) and then spend the afternoon relaxing at **Malpe Beach** (103 km). This option involves more travel but offers a coastal experience.
*   **Evening:** Return to Shringeri or consider staying overnight in Udupi if you want to explore more of the coastal region.

This plan offers a balanced itinerary, but feel free to adjust it based on your interests and pace. Remember to check local timings and accessibility for each spot.
